# fitness-subscription-analytics
A data analytics project that analyze  gym memberships, subscription models, and user engagement trends.

While the overall numbers show a steep decline across the broader timeline—ranging from a peak of 769 down to 46 active users—the behavior shifts after the initial onboarding window. Customer cancellations peak sharply during Months 2–3, indicating a critical early friction point. Beyond Month 4, the rate of cancellation slows down significantly but does not flatten out into a perfect plateau; instead, bubble size metrics confirm a steady, gradual decline in active users through Month 24, indicating that long-term customer retention requires continuous engagement strategies rather than assuming early survivors are permanently retained.
Over the next 12 months (all of 2026), monthly subscription revenue is expected to grow very quickly. It will rise from about $300,000 in January 2026 to around $1,400,000 by January 2027.The chart shows no signs of seasonal changes. Both the past sales (dark blue line) and future estimates (light blue line) form a perfectly smooth curve that bends straight upward. There are regular holiday dips or summer spikes in the data; the growth is steady and continuous.
